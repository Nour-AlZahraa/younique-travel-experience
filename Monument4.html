Why the Parthenon Still Matters

Symbol of Democracy

Built during the rise of Athenian democracy, the Parthenon represents freedom of thought and civic power.

Architectural Perfection

Every curve and column was mathematically calculated to correct visual illusions and create perfect balance.

Global Influence

From museums to modern government buildings, its design inspired architecture across the world.
