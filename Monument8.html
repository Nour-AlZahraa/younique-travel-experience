History of the Tower

The Leaning Tower of Pisa, one of Italy's most iconic landmarks, has fascinated visitors for centuries. Its unique tilt is due to soft ground on one side, but its beauty and history have stood the test of time.

1173

Construction of the tower begins. Designed as a bell tower for Pisa Cathedral, the foundation is laid on soft soil.

14th Century

The tower starts leaning noticeably due to unstable ground. Construction is halted multiple times to allow soil settlement.

16th Century

Construction finally completes after 199 years. The tilt has become a famous feature, drawing curiosity and admiration.

1990-2001

Major restoration and stabilization works are carried out. The tower is now safe for visitors while preserving its tilt.

Today

The Leaning Tower of Pisa attracts millions of tourists yearly. Its architectural beauty, history, and quirky tilt continue to amaze visitors from all over the world.

Visiting the Tower

  • Climb 294 steps to enjoy panoramic views.
  • Buy tickets online to skip long queues.
  • Best photography times: early morning or late afternoon.
  • Nearby attractions: Pisa Cathedral, Baptistery, Cemetery.
